About this game

Enterprise Employer is a small cozy office manager with a retro Windows 98 aesthetic.

  • Meet and hire over 120 characters with each their own personality and office preferences.
  • Build and organise the office for your employees to be happy to earn coins and progress.
  • Make your office feel like home with 100+ furniture and painting options.
  • Expand your office and encounter new puzzle & management challenges.

Relaxing dynamic soundtrack. Special personality system based on the DISC model used by real companies

Expected playtime: 2-3 hours.
